# Photo-referenced museum illustrations

These display images are AI-generated adaptations, not new specimen photographs.
Every delivered image was made with its corresponding real photograph supplied
as an image input to the OpenAI image-generation tool on the date recorded in its generation manifest (2026-09-10 or 2026-09-11). The source
and output were visually inspected to retain material appearance, specimen form
and necessary containment. This visual review is not a chemical analysis or a
claim that generated surface details are measurements.

The original photographs remain separately available in `../collection/`.
In the interface, “AI display image” and “Reference photo” switch between the
two; the Sources tab shows the actual input photo, its photographer/provider,
source page and licence. Generated images are never substituted for the source
evidence. Scale, background and lighting are presentation choices. Electrical
discharge photographs and their adaptations show excited gas, not ordinary gas
colour. No bulk specimens are invented for elements represented only by mineral,
compound, experimental-sample or contextual research photographs.

Each `generation-*.json` array records the final asset, the real input asset,
both SHA-256 hashes, source link, credit, source licence, full prompt, generator,
generated file identifier and visual-review note. These files form the asset
provenance record; `../collection/sources.json` contains original download URLs.

Source photographs retain their individual CC BY 2.0, 3.0, 4.0 or public-domain status.
CC BY-derived display images are distributed as adaptations under the source photograph’s recorded CC BY version,
with the original credit and change notice retained. Public-domain references
are credited as recorded; the project adds no exclusive-rights claim to their
generated adaptations. The repository's software and project-owned-content
licences do not relicense these mixed-source visual assets. No source provider
is claimed to endorse the generated results.

## Six supplementary reference-based treatments

Th and Fr retain the actual vial and material from their photographic input, including the original crop. Fr represents a uranium mineral with trace francium, not bulk francium metal. At, Pa and Po use white-background photographic plates to keep their experimental context intact. At retains its long-exposure dark-field glow; Pa and Po remain monochrome. These are labelled AI presentation adaptations, not new experimental records. Fine details remain subject to generative variation; the unchanged reference is the evidential image. Each supplementary manifest records `sourceKind` and `visualTreatment` alongside the full prompt, actual input and output hashes and source licence.

## Museum-wall additions (2026-09-11)

The revised Rn display isolates the field instrument from the same USGS photograph. It is an interpretive image of radon research equipment, not visible radon gas or an exact instrument schematic. The unchanged field photograph remains accessible. F depicts fluorite, a calcium fluoride mineral, not fluorine gas. Tc, Np, Am and Cf follow their actual reference specimens; Np retains its separate bowl and support, and Cf is not enlarged to imply a bulk specimen.

These generation masters have an opaque light background and remain unchanged for provenance. The interface now uses the separately recorded real-alpha derivatives in `../cutouts/`; their alpha masks remove the outer presentation background without generating new subject pixels. The three experimental plates retain their complete photographic field. See `../cutouts/NOTICE.md` and `sources.json` for processing, source hashes and limitations. The reference-photo mode preserves the original photograph's background and colour. Compressed local static assets and generated 160px grid / 640px inspection variants keep the 118-cell gallery from downloading full images on entry.
