# Element specimen photographs

Seven original photographs from **Chemical Elements — A Virtual Museum**,
https://images-of-elements.com/ (Jumk.de Webprojects).

Each selected source page states **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported**,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/, unless otherwise noted. The selected
images have no additional exception indicated on their pages. Source pages and
original image URLs, retrieval date, SHA-256 and modification records are listed
per file in `sources.json`. Attribution links and the license appear in each
specimen's Photo credits panel. These images retain CC BY 3.0; project AGPL and
CC BY-NC notices do not relicense them.

Original JPEG pixels are retained. CSS scales images to fit the designed case;
no AI reconstruction, recolouring, background removal or shape reconstruction.
The photographed objects are not displayed at a common physical scale.

Cu is native copper; S is natural sulfur; Si is high-purity elemental silicon.
Bi has a surface oxide film. Hg is pure liquid mercury. Br retains its sealed
vial. O is a photograph of glowing oxygen during electrical discharge, not its
ordinary colour. No imagined macroscopic specimen is used for Og; the complete table uses a clearly labelled research-context photograph.

## Full photographic table

The expanded 118-position collection is documented in `collection/NOTICE.md` and
`collection/sources.json`. Photographs of specimens, discharge tubes, minerals,
compounds, experimental samples and research context are labeled separately.
A related photograph for an element without an available specimen is not a claim
that the photograph depicts that element's bulk appearance.
