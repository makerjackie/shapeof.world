# Sourced nuclear synthesis illustrations

These 17 SVG drawings and the live animation are original Shape of the World visual explanations of published fusion-evaporation reactions. They replace generic apparatus placeholders for Md, No, Lr, Rf, Db, Sg, Bh, Hs, Mt, Ds, Rg, Cn, Nh, Fl, Mc, Lv and Og. They are explicitly labelled schematic, not specimen photographs or AI restagings of an imagined metal.

`sources.json` records each chosen beam, target, product isotope, neutron channel, experimental context, scientific source URL and SVG SHA-256. The drawing and live animation use `synthesis-graphic.ts`; the committed assets are static renderings of exactly the same drawing function. Reaction facts are referenced, not copied source artwork. These are representative measured channels, not blanket claims of discovery priority. Nobelium and lawrencium use a 2023 Berkeley experiment report; livermorium uses the 2024 titanium-50 experiment. Dates denote the stated experiment/report, not necessarily discovery or formal recognition.

Brown dots represent protons and teal dots neutrons. The two-dimensional packing, colours, nuclear geometry, distances and timing are explanatory choices, not observed properties or physical trajectory calculations. One successful channel is shown; the drawing omits unsuccessful collisions, gamma emission, experimental yields, equipment and subsequent radioactive decay. The product's mass number initializes the adjacent neutral-atom counter; changing its isotope is independent of the fixed example reaction.

The collection grid uses transparent 160×120 WebP derivatives in `thumb/`,
generated by `scripts/generate-periodic-table-diagram-thumbnails.mjs` with the
tool versions, source/output hashes and recipe recorded in `thumb/sources.json`.
SVGs are rasterized by the recorded Chromium engine to preserve their nested
gradients, then compressed to WebP; no scientific geometry is regenerated.
This avoids downloading all 17 full SVG drawings for tiny cells. The featured
preview and live detail retain the vectors and original scientific data; none of
the source SVGs is cropped, overwritten or replaced as evidence. These derivatives
of the original Shape of the World illustrations carry the same credit and
CC BY-NC 4.0 terms. They stay local compressed static
assets for the existing offline collector, with content hashes in their paths.
