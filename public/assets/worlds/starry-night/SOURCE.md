# The Starry Night painting reproduction

Vincent van Gogh, *The Starry Night* (1889), Museum of Modern Art. Faithful two-dimensional reproduction from Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Van_Gogh_-_Starry_Night_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg . The Commons file page identifies the painting and reproduction with Public Domain Mark 1.0. This reproduction is used in the painting comparison view and, since 2026-10-11, as the pigment source for the moon and eleven closed star volumes. Runtime sampling copies colours into mesh vertices; the invented surface depth and the surrounding village remain an artistic extension. No separate image derivatives are distributed.
