# Fresh cuts atlas

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this world. Illustrative food imagery, not photographs, measured anatomy or histology. Actual tissue patterns and restaurant cuts vary.

Grid: 4 columns by 3 rows; cells follow the atlas coordinates in cow-data.ts. The final cell is an empty plate. Runtime uses one local WebP atlas, 1448 x 1086 pixels, 509870 bytes, serving eleven small plate illustrations together (under the 1 MiB initial image budget). The 362-pixel cells are used at up to 295 CSS pixels. No higher-resolution detail images are preloaded.

Conversion: cwebp, quality 88, method 6; original dimensions and alpha preserved. Source retained in the private Codex generated-images directory. No exclusive copyright claim is made for AI-generated imagery. Google Cow is a separate existing asset and retains its Apache-2.0 notice at /assets/cow/NOTICE.txt.
