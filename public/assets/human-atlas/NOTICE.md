Shape of the World keeps the batched Three.js anatomy renderer, explosion
layout, picking, and BodyParts3D packing. The surrounding UI, bilingual
copy, lighting, and shell integration are rewritten for this site.

## Anatomy data

BodyParts3D 4.0 adult male reference anatomy,
© The Database Center for Life Science, CC BY 4.0.

See `ATTRIBUTION.md` for the full credit, source archive, publication,
and adaptation notes. The geometry is not relicensed under AGPL or the
project's original-content license.

This world is an educational explorer, not a diagnostic or surgical tool.
It represents one adult male reference, not every human structure or variation.
