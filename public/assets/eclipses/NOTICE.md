# Eclipse films and ground plates

`ground.webm`, `close.webm`, and `orbit.webm` are original recordings of the
Shape of the World eclipses renderer. They are not observational footage and
do not contain third-party photographs or videos.

Regenerate with:

```sh
BASE_URL=http://127.0.0.1:<port> node scripts/capture-eclipses-films.mjs
```

`ground-china-day.jpg`, `ground-china-night.jpg`, `ground-europe-day.jpg`,
`ground-europe-night.jpg`, `ground-usa-day.jpg`, and `ground-usa-night.jpg`
are original stills generated with Grok imagegen for this world. They are
illustrative city skylines, not observational photographs. The Sun and Moon
in the experience are drawn by the renderer on top of these plates.
