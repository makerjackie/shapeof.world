NASA media usage policy: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/ . This asset is excluded from the project's AGPL and CC BY-NC licenses.
- Scientific scope: historical composite assembled from two days of visible imagery and an additional day of thermal infrared imagery over the poles. The cloud layer is illustrative and does not represent the weather at the selected simulation date.
- Runtime treatment: sampled as continuous grayscale cloud opacity, lit using the simulated solar direction. No generated satellite imagery is used.
