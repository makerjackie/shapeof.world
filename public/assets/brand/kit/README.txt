Shape of the World / 世界的形状 — logo kit
==========================================

Mark
----
The orb is the emblem. The dark interior is part of the mark; do not lighten it.
On light backgrounds, crop it as a circle so the black sphere reads as a disc.

Lockups
-------
Place the orb to the left of the name. Use:

- 世界的形状                  Chinese
- Shape of the World          English
- 世界的形状 + English line   bilingual

Do not put the line “The World, Playable” inside the lockup. That line is a
tagline for posters and video end cards.

Minimum size next to type: 40 px. Below 24 px, use the wordmark only.

Fonts
-----
Chinese: Noto Serif SC (Source Han Serif SC), SIL Open Font License 1.1
English: Cormorant Garamond, SIL Open Font License 1.1

Do not substitute PingFang, Songti, or other system fonts when making new
lockups.

One-colour print
----------------
mark-mono.png         white light on black
mark-mono-invert.png  black light on white (the dark body disappears)
mark-stamp.png        black disc on white, lights knocked out

Prefer the stamp on stationery. Use invert when you only want the crescent.

Colour
------
Paper  #f1ecdf
Ink    #19231f
Black  #080a0a
Gold   the terminator in the orb
Cyan   the upper-left rim

Do not recolour the orb, add a drop shadow, or place it in a rounded square.
