# Video outro / 视频片尾

- `brand-zh.mp4`, `brand-en.mp4`: fixed brand masters, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 1.8 s, no audio. Do not re-render.
- `brand-credit-zh.mp4`, `brand-credit-en.mp4` (1080×1920) and `brand-credit-*-16x9.mp4` (1920×1080): the same masters with a text-only credit line (制作 MakerJackie / by MakerJackie). The 16:9 files use the documented crop of the master (1080×1080 from y=420, padded with `#080a0a`). Same length and frame rate, no audio.

The credit versions are generated by `scripts/brand-credit/render.mjs` from `credit.json` and `credit.html` in that directory; `manifest.json` there records the source and output SHA-256. Sponsor logos are not part of video outros.

Names, the logo, and the app identity are not licensed with the code or content. See [TRADEMARKS.md](../../../../TRADEMARKS.md).
