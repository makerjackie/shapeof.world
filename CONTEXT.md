# Domain Glossary

- **World**: The smallest playable, authored, and versioned knowledge experience.
- **Category**: A browsable knowledge node. Categories form a controlled directed acyclic graph with one primary parent and optional additional parents.
- **Facet**: A typed, unordered property used for filtering and search, such as difficulty, runtime, evidence level, or experience kind.
- **Path**: An optional editorial route through an ordered list of Worlds. A World may appear in multiple Paths.
