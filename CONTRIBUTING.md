# Contributing / 贡献说明

Shape of the World is a creator-led project. Bug reports, accessibility issues,
translation corrections, rights concerns, and source corrections are welcome.

## Before submitting code or content

Before submitting any code, translation, artwork, documentation, or other
copyrightable material intended for merge, first reach agreement with the
maintainer about scope and contribution terms.

Shape of the World publishes eligible project-controlled material under AGPL for
software and CC BY-NC for original content, and offers separately licensed
commercial editions. To preserve that model, every external copyrightable
contribution must be covered
by an agreement that gives **自由维度（深圳）科技有限公司** sufficient rights
for both the public and commercial versions:

- an individual signs the [Individual CLA](./CLA.md);
- an employer or other rights-owning organization signs the
  [Corporate CLA](./CCLA.md); and
- where personal and employer-owned rights overlap, both agreements may be
  required.

Signing and private recordkeeping follow
[CLA_ACCEPTANCE.md](./CLA_ACCEPTANCE.md). A PR checkbox, typed GitHub comment,
or `Signed-off-by` line does not replace the signed agreement. Until the
templates and signing flow receive qualified PRC legal review, external
copyrightable contributions remain merge-blocked.

Bug reports, rights reports, factual corrections, and ideas remain welcome;
opening an issue or sharing an idea does not transfer copyright.

## Pull requests

Use the repository pull-request template. For an external copyrightable
contribution, a maintainer must record `CLA verified` only after checking:

1. signer identity, authority, and agreement version/hash;
2. the GitHub accounts, PRs, and commits covered by the agreement;
3. employer or commissioning-party rights where applicable; and
4. all third-party and AI-assisted material disclosures.

Do not include signed agreements, residential addresses, identity documents,
private email addresses, signatures, or company seals in a pull request.

## Third-party material

Every contribution must identify the source and exact license of third-party
code, data, models, images, fonts, audio, and other assets. Material without a
traceable, compatible permission record will not be merged.
