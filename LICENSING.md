# Licensing / 许可说明

Shape of the World uses a layered licensing model. Different parts of this
repository have different rights and obligations.

## 1. Project-owned software code

Unless a file or directory carries a more specific notice, software code owned
or controlled by the Shape of the World project is licensed under the
[GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only](./LICENSE) (`AGPL-3.0-only`).
Bundled third-party packages, media, and notices retain their more specific
terms.

Commercial use is allowed under the AGPL. A company does not need to buy a
commercial license when it fully complies with the AGPL, including the source,
notice, and network-use obligations applicable to its particular use.

When a proposed proprietary or closed-source use would otherwise require AGPL
compliance, an organization that wants alternative terms must obtain a separate
written commercial license. The AGPL does not make every internal, independent,
or merely aggregated commercial use subject to source-disclosure obligations.
See [COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md](./COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md).

## 2. Original editorial and visual content

Eligible project-owned editorial text and visual content are licensed under
[CC BY-NC 4.0](./CONTENT_LICENSE.md). Uses outside that license's
`NonCommercial` scope require separate written permission when copyright
permission is legally required.

## 3. Third-party materials

The AGPL and CC BY-NC notices do not relicense third-party code, datasets,
models, images, fonts, audio, embedded services, or derived works. Those items
remain under their upstream terms. File-level or directory-level `LICENSE`,
`NOTICE`, source, and provenance records take precedence.

See [OSS_NOTICES.md](./OSS_NOTICES.md), [DATA_SOURCES.md](./DATA_SOURCES.md),
and the notices stored beside individual assets.

## 4. Brand

No software or content license grants rights to the Shape of the World name,
the Chinese name “世界的形状”, logos, or other brand identifiers. See
[TRADEMARKS.md](./TRADEMARKS.md).

## 5. Contributions

Commercial dual licensing requires the project to retain sufficient rights in
accepted contributions. Every copyrightable code or content contribution
intended for merge therefore requires prior maintainer agreement and an
appropriate contributor agreement. Individuals use [CLA.md](./CLA.md) and
rights-owning organizations use [CCLA.md](./CCLA.md). See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).
