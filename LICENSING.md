Shape of the World is one repository: the web experience, the world runtime and
the authored worlds live here together. Different parts still carry different
rights and obligations.

[GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only](./LICENSE) (`AGPL-3.0-only`).
Bundled third-party packages, media and notices keep their own terms.

Running this software as a network service means offering the corresponding
source to the users of that service. The public repository is that source.

Commercial use is allowed under the AGPL. An organization that wants terms
other than the AGPL must obtain a separate written commercial license; see
[COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md](./COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md). The AGPL does not make every
internal, independent or merely aggregated commercial use subject to
source-disclosure obligations.

## 2. Original editorial and visual content — CC BY-NC 4.0

Eligible original editorial text and visual work created for the project is
licensed under [CC BY-NC 4.0](./CONTENT_LICENSE.md). Uses outside that licence's
`NonCommercial` scope require separate written permission when copyright
permission is legally required.

## 3. Third-party materials

The AGPL and CC BY-NC notices do not relicense third-party code, datasets,
models, images, fonts, audio, embedded services or derived works. Those items
remain under their upstream terms, and file-level or directory-level `LICENSE`,
`NOTICE`, source and provenance records take precedence.

See [OSS_NOTICES.md](./OSS_NOTICES.md), [DATA_SOURCES.md](./DATA_SOURCES.md) and
the notices stored beside individual worlds and assets. Every world must carry
its own source record; the entry rule for new worlds is in
[AGENTS.md](./AGENTS.md).

## 4. Brand

No software or content licence grants rights to the Shape of the World name, the
Chinese name “世界的形状”, the logo or other brand identifiers. See
[TRADEMARKS.md](./TRADEMARKS.md).

## 5. Contributions

Commercial licensing and any future relicensing require the project to hold
sufficient rights in accepted contributions. Every copyrightable code or content
contribution intended for merge therefore needs prior maintainer agreement and
an appropriate contributor agreement. Individuals use [CLA.md](./CLA.md),
rights-owning organizations use [CCLA.md](./CCLA.md), and acceptance records
follow [CLA_ACCEPTANCE.md](./CLA_ACCEPTANCE.md). See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).
