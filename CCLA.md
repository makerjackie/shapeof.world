## 1. Definitions / 定义

**Project / 项目**, **Public Licenses / 公开许可**, and **Contribution / 贡献**
have the meanings stated in [Individual CLA Version 1.0 dated
2026-08-10](./CLA.md), except that a
Contribution under this Agreement is submitted by an Authorized Contributor in
Schedule A within the scope of the Contributor Entity's ownership or control.

“项目”“公开许可”和“贡献”与[个人贡献者许可协议](./CLA.md)中的定义相同，但本协议
项下贡献应由附件 A 所列授权贡献者提交，并限于贡献组织拥有或控制权利的范围。

**Affiliate / 关联方** means an entity that controls, is controlled by, or is
under common control with the Contributor Entity, where control means the power
to direct management or ownership of fifty percent or more of voting interests.

“关联方”指控制贡献组织、受贡献组织控制或与贡献组织受共同控制的组织；“控制”指
有权决定管理方向或持有百分之五十以上的表决权益。

**Effective Date / 生效日** means the later of the dates on which the
Contributor Entity and the Company execute this Agreement, unless the signature
block expressly states another date.

“生效日”指贡献组织与公司完成签署日期中较晚的一日，签署页另有明确约定的除外。

许可相关权利的关联方，授予公司一项**永久、全球、非独占、可转让、免费、免许可费

## 4. Public and commercial licensing / 公开与商业再许可

The Company may license Contributions and derivative works under the applicable
Public Licenses, other open-source or open-content licenses, and commercial,
proprietary, source-available, or closed-source terms. This expressly includes
commercial editions, customer deployments, offline packages, white-label
products, and paid services. Public inclusion in the Project will also be made
available under the applicable Public License. The Company is not required to
accept or continue using any Contribution.

公司可以按照适用的公开许可、其他开源或开放内容许可，以及商业、专有、源码可见或
闭源条款许可贡献及其衍生作品，包括商业版本、客户部署、离线包、白标产品和付费服务。
纳入公开项目的部分也应按照适用的公开许可提供。公司没有必须接受或持续使用任何贡献
的义务。

## 5. Patent license / 专利许可

For patent claims the Contributor Entity or its Affiliates own or control that
would necessarily be infringed by a Contribution alone or by its combination
with the Project as submitted, the Contributor Entity grants the Company a
perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, transferable, royalty-free patent license,
with the right to sublicense, to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell,
import, and otherwise transfer the Contribution and such combinations.

If a recipient of this patent license initiates patent litigation alleging that
a Contribution or the Project as combined with a Contribution infringes a
patent, the patent licenses granted to that recipient for the affected
Contribution terminate when the litigation is filed.

对于贡献组织或其关联方拥有或控制，且因单独实施贡献或按提交时方式将贡献与项目
结合而必然受到实施的专利权利要求，贡献组织授予公司永久、全球、非独占、可转让、
免许可费并可再许可的专利许可，以制造、委托制造、使用、许诺销售、销售、进口和
转让贡献及该等组合。任何专利许可接收方主动提起诉讼，主张贡献或贡献与项目的组合
侵犯专利时，其就相关贡献取得的专利许可自诉讼提起之日起终止。

## 6. Representations and administration / 声明与管理

The Contributor Entity represents that:

1. the signer has authority to bind the Contributor Entity;
2. it and its Affiliates own or control the rights granted for each Contribution;
3. each Authorized Contributor has permission to submit Contributions on its
   behalf and is accurately listed in Schedule A;
4. each third-party portion is conspicuously identified with its source,
   license, and restrictions, and no confidential or commercially
   non-sublicensable material is knowingly included;
5. AI-generated or AI-assisted material is disclosed, and only rights actually
   owned or controlled are granted; and
6. it will promptly update Schedule A and notify the Company if any statement
   above becomes inaccurate.

贡献组织声明并保证：签署人有权代表并约束贡献组织；贡献组织及其关联方拥有或控制
每项贡献所授予的权利；每名授权贡献者均已获得代表组织提交贡献的许可并准确列入
附件 A；所有第三方部分均已醒目标明来源、许可和限制，且不明知包含保密信息或无法
商业再许可的材料；AI 生成或辅助材料已披露，且仅授予实际拥有或控制的权利；附件 A
或上述声明发生变化时将及时更新并通知公司。

Authorized Contributors must also sign the Individual CLA when they retain
personal rights, moral rights, or independently owned material relevant to a
Contribution, or whenever the Company reasonably requests it.

授权贡献者对贡献仍享有个人权利、著作人身权或独立拥有的材料时，或者公司合理要求
时，还必须签署个人贡献者许可协议。

## 7. No support obligation; disclaimer / 无支持义务与免责声明

The Contributor Entity is not required to support Contributions. Except for the
express representations above and to the extent permitted by law, Contributions
are provided “AS IS”, without warranties of merchantability, fitness for a
particular purpose, or non-infringement.

贡献组织无义务为贡献提供支持。除上述明确声明及法律另有强制规定外，贡献按“现状”
提供，不附带适销性、特定用途适用性或不侵权等保证。

## 8. Records, term, and assignment / 记录、期限与转让

The Company may privately retain signed agreements, authority evidence, the
Agreement version and hash, Schedule A history, and covered contribution
identifiers. The Contributor Entity may stop future Contributions, but rights
already granted remain irrevocable. The Company may assign this Agreement and
the granted rights to an affiliate or successor that acquires or continues the
Project. Amendments must be in writing; unenforceable provisions will be limited
or replaced to preserve the Parties' intent.

公司可以私密保存已签协议、授权证明、协议版本及哈希、附件 A 历史和所覆盖贡献的
标识。贡献组织可以停止未来贡献，但已经授予的权利仍不可撤销。公司可以将本协议及
其权利转让给承接或继续运营项目的关联方或继受方。任何修改须采用书面形式；部分
条款不可执行时，应在最大程度保留双方原意的前提下予以限缩或替换。

submitted to the competent People's Court at the Company's domicile. The

本协议适用中华人民共和国法律。协商无法解决的争议，提交公司住所地有管辖权的
人民法院处理。中英文文本为同一协议；如有不一致，以中文文本为准。
