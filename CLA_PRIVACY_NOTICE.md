## Information processed / 处理的信息

Depending on the agreement and signing method, the Company may process:

- legal and public names, GitHub accounts, email, address, country or region;
- employer or entity name, registration data, job title, and authority evidence;
- signatures, company seals, e-signature certificates, timestamps, IP address,
  and signing audit logs where supplied by the signing service;
- agreement version and hash, covered pull requests and commits, and later
  rights or provenance notices; and
- identity-document details only when reasonably necessary to verify authority.

根据协议及签署方式，公司可能处理：法定姓名和公开署名、GitHub 账号、邮箱、地址、
国家或地区；雇主或组织名称、登记信息、职务和授权证明；签名、公司印章、电子签名
证书、时间戳、IP 地址和签署审计日志；协议版本及哈希、覆盖的 PR 和 Commit 及后续
权利或来源通知；以及在合理必要时用于核验身份或权限的证件信息。

## Purposes and methods / 目的与方式

Information is used only to conclude and perform contributor agreements,
verify identity and authority, associate agreements with Contributions, manage
open and commercial licensing, prevent rights disputes, and satisfy legal or
audit obligations. Processing may be manual or electronic and must follow
purpose limitation and data minimization.

信息仅用于订立和履行贡献者协议、核验身份和权限、将协议与贡献关联、管理公开与
商业许可、防范权利争议以及履行法律或审计义务。处理可以采用人工或电子方式，并应
遵循目的明确和最小必要原则。

## Disclosure and public records / 提供与公开

Complete agreements and identity evidence are not stored in the public
repository. Access is limited to authorized Company personnel and, when
necessary, the signing provider, legal or audit advisers, courts, regulators,
or a Project successor bound to protect the information. A public CLA registry,
if created, contains only the contributor's chosen public name, GitHub account,
agreement type, version, and verification date.

完整协议和身份材料不存放在公开仓库。仅限公司授权人员，以及在必要情况下的签署
服务商、法律或审计顾问、法院、监管机关或承担保护义务的项目继受方访问。未来如
建立公开 CLA 登记表，仅记录贡献者选择的公开姓名、GitHub 账号、协议类型、版本和
核验日期。

## Retention and security / 保存与安全

Records are retained only for the period reasonably necessary to prove and
administer the granted rights, comply with law, and resolve disputes. The
Company should periodically review necessity and securely delete information no
longer needed, while preserving the minimum agreement evidence required for the
duration of licensed rights. Reasonable access controls, encrypted transport,
backups, and audit records should be used.

记录仅在证明和管理授权、履行法律义务及解决争议所合理必要的期间内保存。公司应
定期复核必要性并安全删除不再需要的信息，同时在许可权利存续期间保留证明协议所需
的最少证据，并采用合理的访问控制、加密传输、备份和审计措施。

## Contributor rights / 贡献者权利

Subject to applicable law, a contributor may request information, access,
correction, supplementation, or deletion of personal information. Deletion does
not revoke rights already granted under a contributor agreement, and the
Company may retain minimum records when legally necessary to prove those rights
or handle disputes.

在适用法律范围内，贡献者可以请求说明、查阅、复制、更正、补充或删除个人信息。
删除个人信息不撤销贡献者协议已经授予的权利；为证明相关权利或处理争议确有法律
必要时，公司可以保留最少记录。

This notice and the signing workflow must receive qualified PRC legal review
before production use. 本告知与签署流程在正式启用前应由具备资质的中国律师复核。
