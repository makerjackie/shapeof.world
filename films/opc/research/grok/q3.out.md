Latest reference year is 2024, released September 29, 2026. A nonemployer has no paid employees, is subject to federal income tax, and has receipts of at least $1,000 ($1 in construction). Receipts are gross revenue before expenses. One person can have more than one establishment, and the count includes partnerships and corporations with no payroll, not only sole proprietors.

2023 receipts in the US file are 1,752,978,019 and 2024 receipts are 1,861,386,279, both in thousands of dollars. QuickFacts prints the same establishment counts. From 2023 to 2024 the count rose 5.1% and nominal receipts rose 6.2%. Those two percentages are calculated from the file totals.

## 2. IRS Statistics of Income, Schedule C

Latest detailed tables are tax year 2023, source line "IRS, Statistics of Income Division, Sole Proprietorship Returns 2023, March 2026." Money amounts in the files are thousands of dollars. A return can also include more than one business.

- Returns: 31,125,909.
- Business receipts: $2.063 trillion. This is revenue.
- Returns with net income: 21,411,462, which is 68.8% of returns.
- Net income of those profitable returns: $530.8 billion.
- Deficits, the sum of losses: $153.6 billion.
- Net income less deficit: $377.2 billion. This is aggregate profit after losses.
- Calculated average of net income less deficit: $12,119 per return.
- Calculated average net income among returns with net income: $24,790.
