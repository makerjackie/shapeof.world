# Film6 sources: 两千年中国人口 / 两千年世界人口

Rules:
- The line only connects real data points. Gaps longer than 25 years are drawn as straight dashed lines.
- Registered counts (户口登记) and scholarly estimates (学者估算) are labelled as such on screen.
- Every death toll is a published estimate, shown with its range.
- Maps are schematic (疆域示意). Outlines come from aourednik/historical-basemaps (GPL-3.0).
  - Splits that the dataset lacks are drawn by hand around the regimes' seats: 群雄割据, 十六国, 隋末, 五代十国, 明末, 安史, 太平天国.
  - 元 / 明 / 清 include 西藏 and 台湾, as in the standard Chinese historical atlas.
  - After 1912 (China) and 1900 (world), no political borders are drawn.

## China film: series
Points are the same as Film5: 《汉书》《后汉书》《晋书》《隋书》《通典》《明史》, 清实录, 葛剑雄 / 吴松弟 / 曹树基《中国人口史》, and 国家统计局 1949–2025. Two additions:
- **263, 766.7 万:** the three kingdoms' registered totals combined. Source: zh.wikipedia 中国人口史, citing 《三国志》注 and 《晋书》.
  - 魏, 263 (景元四年): 66.3 万户, 443.3 万口
  - 蜀, 263: 28 万户, 94 万口
  - 吴, 280 (天纪四年): 52.3 万户, 230 万口
- The 2100 value is the UN WPP 2024 medium variant.
