# 松溪入画 / Songxi: Into the Ink

An original painting and its authored spatial extension for Shape of the World. The owner approved the 2D master on 2026-10-12. The extended world contains the pine pavilion, river banks, a real masonry arch opening, boats, a village with a traversable gatehouse, volumetric peaks and ridges, and grounded hillside terraces; the rejected floating waterfalls have been removed. The website and continuous films share the same renderer. The current horizontal and vertical films have been exported with a 22.8-second authored flight plus the existing 1.8-second brand outro, totalling 24.6 seconds each. They preserve the preceding version's scene geometry and panoramic composition while revising the return turn and viewing time. The painting remains available for comparison on the website, and the films move directly from the continuous 3D panorama to the brand outro. Buildings and vegetation omitted from the original viewpoint are creative additions, not recovered historical geometry.

Near and middle ground use meshes, painted surface maps and foliage clusters distributed in three dimensions. All mountain layers use closed meshes, including the background. The current geometry distinguishes slender spires, long ridges with several uneven summits, lower shoulders and broken crags with stepped rock shelves. Summit spacing, profile and orientation vary between groups. Foliage remains painted cards distributed in 3D branches. The earlier horizon asset is retained as source material but is no longer rendered. Two banks of low hills, seven groups of cultivated terraces and eight small pine groves connect the village to the peaks; the widening river banks use smooth transitions. The extended valley is an authored world, not a claim to have recovered unique hidden geometry from one image. Free movement is simplified and permits traversing geometry; it is not a drone physics simulator.

The front section starts above a moored boat, passes through an open pavilion, drops beneath the stone bridge, follows village lanes through a gatehouse, passes under a covered bridge and approaches a riverside teahouse. The six village building types use distinct geometry: a gabled home, balcony teahouse, stepped firewall house, open workshop, moon-gate courtyard and reed-roof cottage. The lower final viewpoint and shallow downward angle preserve foreground, middle ground and distant mountain layers: a nearby pine and rooted rocks frame the left, a dominant peak anchors the right, and the travelled river and village lie between them. The owner has acknowledged this scenery improvement; acceptance of camera comfort and viewing time remains separate. Position and aim are independently authored. The flight retains 35 scene units per second for the first 9.5 seconds, takes a monotonic short rightward gaze arc from 9.2 to 18 seconds with a peak yaw rate of approximately 24.7 degrees per second, and eases translation to 2 scene units per second. From 18 to 22.8 seconds the gimbal holds a fixed direction during 4.8 seconds of slow movement. World-up remains fixed and roll stays zero. Local continuity and encoded-frame review are complete; the owner's response to the revised camera is pending.

Current local and film evidence is recorded under `output/shape-video/songxi-calm-return-20261013/`: full `pnpm check` passed, along with eight flight tests and 23 world-check tests. All four Chinese/English desktop/mobile browser reviews reported no errors; live playback took approximately 23.03 seconds. Native horizontal and vertical captures each contain 684 consecutive frames at 30 fps, and both 24.6-second encodes passed full decoding. Encoded samples covering the passages, turn, panorama hold and brand transition were inspected. These records establish local and film verification, not owner approval; actual deployment is recorded separately in that directory's `release.json`. The preceding 20-second version's evidence remains under `output/shape-video/songxi-shanshui-20261013/`.

Assets are generated for this project with the built-in image_gen tool. Source hashes, conversions, prompts and rights are next to the assets in `public/assets/worlds/ink-landscape/songxi-v1/`; the earlier original rock brush retains its own source record. Three.js and its Reflector/geometry utilities use the repository's existing dependency and license. No external painting scan, scene or model is copied.

The owner rejected the previous 26-second expansion on 2026-10-12 for camera wobble, water appearance, painting mismatch and unsupported waterfalls/pavilions. Subsequent revisions introduced a newly generated flat brush texture rather than repeating pictures of whole rocks, softer broken reflections, actual building foundations and a forward river-valley route. The master remains the owner-approved art reference. Later feedback on scenery is scoped separately from acceptance of camera movement and timing.

On 2026-10-13 the owner also rejected the steady revision’s pacing and scenery: the view after nine seconds contained too much empty water, repeated houses, and missed the boat and pavilion. The subsequent 18-second revision introduced distinct landmarks and building types, but its low shrine approach did not provide the requested panoramic return. The following 22-second flight, released as `2026.10.13.3`, was rejected for the mismatch between its high panorama and the approved painting, the abrupt comparison cut, and the complete 25.8-second length. Its evidence remains under `output/shape-video/songxi-panorama-20261013/`. For `2026.10.13.4`, the owner explicitly said the scene was much better but found the return turn dizzying and the panorama too brief, and allowed a film longer than 20 seconds. That feedback accepts the scenery improvement, not the camera pacing; it must not be recorded as rejection of the entire version. The new 24.6-second films have completed local and encoded-frame review. No new listening review was performed, and owner acceptance of the revised camera remains pending, with deployment evidence recorded separately.
